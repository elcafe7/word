Welcome to Word

Word shows scripture one word at a time, so your eyes stay still and you read at a steady pace.

Pick a verse. Tap or click the passage at the top of the card, then choose a book, chapter and verse, or type a reference such as 1 John 3:16.

Set the speed. Drag the words-per-minute slider under Play.

Choose a mode. In Settings, you can show or hide the reference bar. Minimalist mode hides everything except the word, the passage and Play.

Open this again any time from Settings › How Word works.
